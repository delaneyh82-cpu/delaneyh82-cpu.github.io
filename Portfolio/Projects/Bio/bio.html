About Me

I am currently a student at Central Michigan University, majoring in Art with a concentration in Graphic Design. I work on campus for University Recreation as a student graphic designer, making graphics and flyers for both social media and print.

I am a designer that loves to experiment with styles–I go outside the box and adapt to each unique situation I'm presented with. I like to think of each design as a puzzle; I have the information I need to make it, it's just a matter of finding where I'll place each piece.

I am at heart a creative, taking part in multiple forms of expression like fiber arts, theatre, literature, and music. As such, I'm in an international music fraternity, Sigma Alpha Iota, where I hold two positions on our eboard.

If you like what you see here, please reach out to me!
